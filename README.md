VELICHAM

Velicham is an advanced, AI-driven dark web threat actor de-anonymization and threat mitigation platform designed for modern law enforcement and cybersecurity agencies.
Traditional investigations often expose human officers to severe physical and digital dangers while struggling to keep pace with volatile, shifting criminal networks.
Velicham shifts the paradigm from reactive monitoring to proactive threat mitigation by automating intelligence gathering, network mapping, and evidence preservation.
At its core, the platform utilizes an autonomous undercover AI agent powered by ultra-low-latency Groq LLM inference to simulate naive buyer personas and seamlessly deploy trackable honeytrap links during real-world negotiations.
When a threat actor interacts with these links, their Tor anonymity is bypassed to capture critical IP addresses and device metadata.
This is backed by a custom AI stylometry engine for linguistic fingerprinting and author attribution across multiple aliases, a cloud-native Neo4j Aura database for real-time relational graph mapping, and an evidence module that generates tamper-proof, court-compliant HTML snapshots for federal legal standards.
